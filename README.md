# My Favorite Movie

## About the Movie

My favorite movie is **Someone Great**. It is a romantic comedy movie about Jenny, a woman who experiences a breakup before moving to another city. She spends one last unforgettable day with her two best friends.

### Why I Like It

I like this movie because:

- The story is relatable and meaningful.
- The friendship between the characters is inspiring.
- The movie has funny and emotional moments.
- It teaches an important lesson about friendship, love, and moving on.
